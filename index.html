Dermlink Landing Page Concepts

Design directions for Dermlink, an asynchronous telemedicine platform. Concepts 01–03 explore the consumer landing page; concepts 04–05 explore a separate physician-facing specialist-network page for concierge medicine. Each concept commits to a distinct aesthetic.

Clinical Serenity

Warm

Soft, organic gradients with warm terracotta and peach tones paired with deep teal accents. Typography-forward with Instrument Serif headlines. Evokes trust and approachability while maintaining medical professionalism.
